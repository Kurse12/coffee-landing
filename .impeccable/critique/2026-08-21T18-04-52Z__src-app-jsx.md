## Priority Issues

**[P1] Nav social icons break character on the very first frame, unconditionally.** `Nav.jsx:126-137` (desktop) and `Nav.jsx:186-201` (mobile) render Instagram/TikTok buttons that are visible from first load and route straight to `abrir('redes')` → HojaDemo's "the cafetería doesn't exist" reveal. **Why it matters:** this is the identical failure mode the Reservar fix just solved — an immediate fiction-break before Origen, Ritual, or Voces have built any case — except it was never applied here, and social icons are a more habitual, lower-friction tap than a CTA button. Product Principle 1 ("anything that reveals the scaffolding weakens the proof") is violated on the first screen a visitor can see. **Fix:** remove the social icons from the persistent header (they already exist in Cierre's footer) or defer them the same way Reservar was deferred. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`.

**[P2] Menu photos over-fetch on the exact device PRODUCT.md names as most likely.** All 14 hotlinked Unsplash images request a fixed `w=1200&h=1500` (`media.js:25`) regardless of the card's actual rendered width (`aspect-[4/5]`, `CartaCompleta.jsx:327`), with no `srcset`/`sizes`. **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md's Operating Context explicitly names "a mid-range Android, in a WhatsApp in-app browser" as the likely real-world viewing condition — this is real data cost and load time on the audience the page itself calls out. **Fix:** request Unsplash's own responsive sizing params scaled to the card's actual display width, or add `sizes`/`srcset`. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`.

**[P2] CartaCompleta's top-level category view still exceeds the page's own chunking discipline.** Café (6 items) and Pastelería (5 items) both render above the ≤4-item working-memory guideline in one continuous view; the subgroup fix helps but the divider is small, uppercase 13px text (`CartaCompleta.jsx:313`) easy to miss on a fast mobile scroll. **Why it matters:** the menu is the one decision point on the page where the visitor is meant to actually choose something, and it's the one place item-count exceeds every other section (Ritual: 3, Locales: 3, Nav: 4). **Fix:** stronger subgroup visual separation (e.g. a visible divider or count badge), or reconsider where the tab boundary sits. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

**[P2] Ritual's three full-viewport pinned cards risk a fatigue valley right before the sections that build the case.** `Ritual.jsx:115` runs three `min-h-[100dvh]` pinned cards between Origen's emotional payoff and Locales/Voces — the sections that actually do the persuading. **Why it matters:** this sits at exactly the point in the emotional journey where momentum toward "reach out" should be building, not stalling on a long forced scroll of process detail. **Fix:** shorten on mobile, or verify actual scroll time on a real mid-range Android before deciding whether to trim further. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize` (pacing) or `/impeccable layout`.

**[P3] Small token/CSS drift remaining.** `CartaCompleta.jsx:339` (item name, `text-lg`) and `:342` (price, `text-base`) sit below DESIGN.md's documented Title tier (`text-xl`–`text-2xl`, DESIGN.md:172-173). Separately, `index.css:234-239` still sets a 12px `.leaflet-popup-content-wrapper` radius — a third corner radius that violates the Two-Shape Rule — but `Mapa.jsx` only uses `Tooltip`, never `Popup`, so it's currently inert dead CSS rather than a live violation; it will silently mis-style the first future contributor who adds a Leaflet popup. **Fix:** bump CartaCompleta's item typography to match Title tier; delete the unused Popup CSS rule. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.
