## Overall Impression

The copy and the small-scale craft (HojaDemo's focus trap, the Locales sticky-map fix, contrast-driven color decisions with measured numbers at point of use) are the real thing — this genuinely reads as authored for a Buenos Aires café, not a template. But the piece's single biggest selling point on paper — the shader-driven hero — isn't running, which means the page currently undersells itself relative to its own PRODUCT.md pitch. The second-biggest issue is sequencing: the most prominent CTA on the page breaks the page's fiction on the very first tap, before any of the backstory/menu/testimonial content has had a chance to build the case first.

## What's Working

- **`HojaDemo.jsx`** — focus trapped and returned to the exact trigger element, `aria-labelledby` deliberately pointed at the heading with a documented reason, and copy that owns the fiction directly rather than dodging it. A hard problem (dead-end demo CTAs) solved with real care.
- **`Locales.jsx` mobile-map reflow** — a documented real bug (map living below the fold when it needed to react to taps) fixed architecturally (`sticky` sharing a scroll container with the list), not patched over with CSS.
- **Contrast-driven color decisions** — `accent-soft` vs. `accent` swaps are justified with measured contrast numbers at the point of use, matching DESIGN.md's Neon Red rule precisely rather than applied by feel.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The hero shader — the page's stated differentiator — is dead code.** `FondoCrema.jsx`/`Crema.jsx` are unused; `Hero.jsx` has no Three.js reference despite PRODUCT.md leading its positioning section with this exact capability. **Why it matters:** a prospect who reads the README (which PRODUCT.md says documents "the reason a token was chosen") and then sees a plain photo hero catches the gap immediately — the thing being sold as unique isn't visible. **Fix:** re-wire `FondoCrema` into `Hero.jsx` (it was clearly built for exactly this), or if the removal was deliberate, update PRODUCT.md/DESIGN.md's positioning and North Star to stop claiming it. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden` (or `/impeccable shape` first if the removal was intentional and the spec needs to change instead).

**[P1] The primary hero CTA is a trapdoor into "this is fake."** `Hero.jsx:103`'s `Reservar` — the single most prominent button on the page — opens HojaDemo and immediately reveals the cafetería doesn't exist, before a first-time visitor has scrolled past the backstory, menu, or testimonials meant to build the case first. **Why it matters:** Product Principle 1 names this exact failure ("Anything that reveals the scaffolding weakens the proof") as the worst outcome, and the highest-traffic CTA is the one most likely to trigger it. **Fix:** let the hero CTA deep-link into the page (scroll to Locales/Cierre) rather than opening HojaDemo immediately, reserving the earliest fiction-break for later in the scroll. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable shape`.

**[P2] Menu category views exceed the page's own chunking discipline.** Café (6 items) and Pastelería (5 items) in `CartaCompleta.jsx` show simultaneously, above the ≤4-item working-memory guideline and inconsistent with the rest of the page (Ritual = 3 steps, Locales = 3 cards, Nav = 4 links). **Why it matters:** the visitor deciding "what do I order" faces more live options at once than anywhere else on the page. **Fix:** sub-group by 3-4 (e.g. split Café into hot/cold) or add a secondary chunk cue (divider/count badge) so 6 doesn't read as one undifferentiated block. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable layout`.

**[P2] Inconsistent ARIA pattern for structurally identical UI.** `Locales.jsx:86` explicitly reasons its way to `aria-current` for a single-select toggle group (with a comment explaining why `aria-pressed` is wrong there); `CartaCompleta.jsx:234` uses `aria-pressed` for the exact same pattern. **Why it matters:** a screen-reader user tabbing through the four category filters hears "not pressed" on three buttons simultaneously — the precise confusion the Locales comment argues against. **Fix:** align `CartaCompleta` to `aria-current` (or a proper `role="tablist"`/`aria-selected` pattern). **Suggested command:** `/impeccable audit`.

**[P3] Documented tokens have drifted from implementation in five confirmed spots.** Detector-confirmed: `Nav.jsx:90` (19px), `Voces.jsx:48` (1.4rem), `index.css:218` (11px, documented intentional bump for Leaflet attribution legibility), `index.css:125` (4px focus ring), `index.css:235` (12px Leaflet popup). Plus DESIGN.md's `hero-display` clamp (`3.6rem, 17vw, 8rem`) doesn't match what `Hero.jsx:90` actually renders (`3.6rem, 13vw, 6.4rem`). **Why it matters:** PRODUCT.md brands "a README that documents rationale" as part of the pitch; three-plus confirmed drifts (plus the orphaned shader) undercut that specific claim if a prospect checks the docs against the code. **Fix:** either fold the drifted values into DESIGN.md's scale as intentional exceptions (with inline `impeccable-disable` comments where deliberate, like the 11px case) or bring the code back in line. **Suggested command:** `/impeccable document` (regenerate DESIGN.md from current code) or `/impeccable polish`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer):** The single most prominent, most-styled button on the entire page — the hero's `Reservar` (`Hero.jsx:103`) — is a guaranteed "this is fake" reveal on first tap. Jordan came to judge "would I be happy if my shop looked like this," and the very first interactive gesture available answers "no, it's a demo" before Jordan has seen the backstory, menu, or testimonials that are supposed to build the case first.

**Riley (Stress Tester):** Riley will click Hero `Reservar`, Nav `Reservar` (desktop and mobile), Cierre's WhatsApp CTA, and both Instagram/TikTok icons in Nav and footer — five distinct visual entry points that all resolve to one of exactly two identical `HojaDemo` copy variants. Within the first minute of probing, the page's convincing individual sections reveal themselves as a thin shell around one fallback screen, undercutting the "range" the piece is trying to demonstrate.
